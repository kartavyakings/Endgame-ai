Chess.com
The dominant platform — 100M+ users
✅ Advantages
  • Largest user base globally
  • Best UI/UX in the industry
  • Best email marketing system
  • Community features: News, Articles, Blogs, Forums, Polls, Clubs, Coaches, Chess TV, Daily Streak
  • Major events: Speed Chess Championship, Champions Chess Tour, Chess.com Open, Global Championship
  • Online tournaments: Titled Tuesday, Bullet Brawl, Arena Kings, 3+0 Thursday, PogChamps
  • Community events: Rapid Opening Roulette, Untitled Tuesday, Prediction contests with prizes
  • Chess24 acquisition boosted event coverage
  • Best learning: Game Review, Puzzles, Lesson Library, Chessable & AimChess integration, AI Coach
❌ Disadvantages
  • Prize tournaments almost exclusively for titled players
  • Untitled players (U1000, U1500, U2000) are largely ignored
  • Biased system where titled players enter "untitled" events and dominate
  • Growing monopoly backlash in the chess community
  • Community feedback widely ignored (Reddit, forums)
Lichess
Open-source charity — free for everyone
✅ Advantages
  • Completely free — no paywalls
  • Best analysis tools with Stockfish engine
  • Best evaluation bars & move suggestions
  • Best Opening Explorer & large database
  • Organizers' favourite platform to host tournaments
  • Variety of puzzles & community study sections
  • Active in tournament coverage
  • Take Take Take collaboration expanding their ecosystem
❌ Disadvantages
  • Outdated UI/UX — not competitive or esports-feeling
  • Limited branding compared to Chess.com
  • Limited community & social features
  • No video educational content
  • Limited social media presence & influencer collabs
  • Rating inflation perception issue
Take Take Take
Magnus Carlsen's platform — expanding into Playzone
✅ Advantages
  • Best UI/UX — modern, esports-feeling
  • Magnus Carlsen as founder = instant credibility
  • Attractive live tournament streams
  • Creator-focused ecosystem with fan clubs & challenges
  • Proper social feed with leaderboard challenges
  • Lichess collaboration for infrastructure & matchmaking
  • Attractive mobile-first app design
❌ Disadvantages
  • Chess.com ended Titled Tuesday broadcast collaboration — huge audience loss
  • No learning content whatsoever
  • No web experience — desktop users excluded
  • Board themes less attractive than competitors
